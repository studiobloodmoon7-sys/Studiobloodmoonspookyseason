Our story

A little local idea that got very big.

Family-run in Stoughton

Built with family, fueled by community.

Stuffed Bagels quickly became one of those places people were willing to drive for. Early weekends regularly sold out, with the Brickson family and extended family pitching in behind the scenes.

Local matters here.
In 2026, co-owner Holly Brickson also launched Stoughton’s Detour Dining Club passport to help drive customers to local businesses affected by road construction.

This concept site puts that same personality front and center: straightforward, warm, local, and unmistakably Stoughton.
